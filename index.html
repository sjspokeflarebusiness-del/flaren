// FAQ

Questions?
Answered.

Do I need an account?

No. The free version works entirely in your browser. Your data stays on your device.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage. If you sign in, it's also backed up on our server.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. Settings → Export data. Save the file. Import it later on any device.

What is the workout tracker?

Six levels from Beginner to Elite. Pick your level, log what you did, and grow a flame. Honest streaks — miss a day and it resets.

Does the pill reminder replace my doctor?

No. It is a reminder tool, not medical advice. Always follow your doctor's instructions and never stop or change medication without consulting a physician.

Will you steal my passwords?

Never. Flaren only stores links. It never asks for passwords. It only opens the official service in a new tab.
